OUR ACTIVITIES

INCLUDE

National Level Events & Festivals

RCSC hosts the annual Rajshahi College National Science Fest, bringing students from across the country together to compete, create, and celebrate science.

Regular Workshops & Seminars

RCSC regularly holds workshops and seminars on science, technology, and research, from hands-on skill-building sessions to talks by experts and guest speakers.

Research & Project Teams

RCSC innovates with student-led science and technology projects, fostering hands-on research and team collaboration across multiple fields.

Quark, our annual magazine, showcases student articles, research, and achievements, capturing RCSC's science and innovation journey throughout the year.
