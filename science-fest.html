About the Event

A Decade of Scientific Excellence

The Rajshahi College National Science Fest is RCSC's most prestigious annual event — a grand celebration of science, technology, and innovation held at the historic Rajshahi College campus.

Now in its 10th edition, the fest brings together students from schools, colleges, and universities across Bangladesh to compete, collaborate, and showcase their scientific talent across multiple disciplines.

From thrilling science olympiads and project exhibitions to cultural science shows and workshops — the Science Fest is a multi-day celebration that leaves a lasting impression on every participant.

What to Expect

  • Competitions across physics, chemistry, biology & more
  • Science project exhibition & innovation showcase
  • Workshops & sessions by distinguished speakers
  • Quiz competitions open to students nationwide
  • Grand prize ceremony & certificates for all participants
