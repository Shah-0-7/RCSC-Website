Who We Are

Rajshahi College Science Club (RCSC), a co-curricular club of Rajshahi College, has started its journey to promote science and technology awareness among students. "Igniting Scientific Curiosity" — bearing this motto, RCSC strives to cultivate a generation of curious minds who push the boundaries of knowledge and innovation.

RCSC provides a platform for students to explore disciplines such as physics, chemistry, biology, computer science, astronomy, robotics, and more — through workshops, competitions, seminars, and collaborative projects. We believe every student has the potential to change the world through science.

Our Motive

At RCSC, our core belief is that science belongs to everyone. We exist to democratize access to scientific thinking and exposure for the students of Rajshahi College — regardless of background or prior knowledge. We want every student to leave college with not just a certificate, but a love for discovery and a habit of questioning the world.

Through hands-on experience, mentorship, and community events, we build the scientists, engineers, and thinkers of tomorrow — right here in Rajshahi.
