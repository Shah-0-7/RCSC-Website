About the Competition

What is DevSprint?

DevSprint is RCSC's flagship app-building competition designed to push developers beyond their comfort zones. Over the course of two intense weeks, participating teams will ideate, design, build, and present a fully functional application.

Whether you're building a web app, mobile app, or cross-platform solution — DevSprint welcomes all stacks and frameworks. The focus is on execution, creativity, and real-world impact.

Teams will be evaluated on innovation, technical implementation, UI/UX design, and a final demo presentation to a panel of judges.

Why Participate?

  • Build a portfolio-worthy project under real constraints
  • Collaborate with talented teammates & grow together
  • Get recognition from RCSC and the wider tech community
  • Win exciting prizes & certificates of participation
  • Network with like-minded developers across Rajshahi

How It Works

From idea to final demo — here's your DevSprint journey

1

Registration & Team Formation

Phase 1 — TBA

Upcoming

Register individually or as a team (2–4 members). Solo participants will be matched with teammates. All stacks welcome — web, mobile, cross-platform.

2

Kickoff & Theme Reveal

Phase 2 — Day 1

Day 1

The competition theme/problem statement is revealed at the kickoff event. Teams begin brainstorming, wireframing, and setting up their development environment.

3

Build Sprint

Phase 3 — Week 1 & 2

2 Weeks

The core build phase. Teams design, develop, and iterate on their application. Regular check-in posts on the official group keep everyone on track. Mentors may be available for guidance.

4

Submission Deadline

Phase 4 — Day 14

Deadline

Submit your project repository, a short demo video, and a brief project description. No late submissions accepted. Make sure your app is functional and documented.

5

Demo Day & Judging

Phase 5 — Finals

Grand Finale

Top teams present their apps live to a panel of judges. Evaluated on innovation, technical quality, UI/UX, and presentation. Winners announced with prizes and certificates.

What You Can Build

Competition Tracks

🌐

Web Application

Build a full-stack or front-end web app using any framework — React, Vue, Next.js, Django, Laravel, or anything you love.

Suggested Stacks

React · Next.js · Vue · Django · Laravel

📱

Mobile Application

Create a native or cross-platform mobile app for Android or iOS. Focus on UX, performance, and real-world usefulness.

Suggested Stacks

Flutter · React Native · Kotlin · Swift

🤖

AI / Smart App

Integrate AI, ML, or data science into your app. Build something smart — a classifier, a recommender, a chatbot, or more.

Suggested Stacks

Python · TensorFlow · Gemini API · LangChain

Evaluation

Judging Criteria

💡

Innovation

25 pts

Originality of the idea, creative use of technology, and how well it addresses a real problem.

⚙️

Technical Implementation

25 pts

Code quality, architecture, use of appropriate tools, and overall technical execution.

🎨

UI/UX Design

25 pts

Visual design, user experience, intuitiveness, and overall polish of the interface.

🎤

Presentation

25 pts

Clarity of the demo, how well the team communicates the value and vision of their product.
